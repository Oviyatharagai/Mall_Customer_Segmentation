Project Title: Mall Customer Segmentation

Problem Statement:
Malls and retail businesses collect large amounts of customer data through membership cards, including details like age, gender, annual income, and spending score. However, without proper analysis, this data remains underutilized, making it difficult for businesses to understand distinct customer groups and design targeted marketing strategies.
The objective of this project is to apply unsupervised machine learning techniques, specifically K-Means Clustering, to segment mall customers into distinct groups based on their annual income and spending score. This segmentation will help the mall's marketing team identify customer categories (e.g., high income–low spenders, low income–high spenders, target customers, etc.) and design personalized marketing campaigns, improve customer retention strategies, and optimize resource allocation for promotions.

Objectives:
Analyze customer demographic and spending data to identify patterns.
Apply K-Means clustering to group customers into meaningful segments.
Visualize clusters to interpret customer behavior.
Provide actionable business insights based on segmentation results.

Dataset: 
Mall_Customers.csv (Kaggle) — contains CustomerID, Gender, Age, Annual Income (k$), Spending Score (1–100),Marital Status,Proffession,Family Size,Online Shopping Score,Loyalty Years
